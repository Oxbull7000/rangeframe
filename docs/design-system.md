# RangeFrame Design System

## Direction

Vantic-inspired kinetic Solana product language: centered motion headline, compact floating nav, coded product preview, deep dark surfaces, blue active states, and large typographic confidence.

## Palette

- Background: `#151515`
- Surface: `#202020`
- Raised surface: `#292929`
- Deep surface: `#111111`
- Text: `#F8F8F8`
- Muted text: `#C2C8D2`
- Dim text: `#7F8590`
- Border: `#444444`
- Accent: `#3B82F6`
- Destructive: `#F43F46`

## Bans

No background images, floating coins, Solana purple glow, fake yield copy, generic dashboard hero, random gradients, or dead launch buttons.
